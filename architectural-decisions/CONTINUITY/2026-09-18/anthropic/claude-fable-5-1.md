## 1. What M1 is, as built (proven live: desktop Chrome, iOS Safari, prod)

Four-node relay: aic-client (browser) ⇄ aic-server (ws-server) ⇄ xai-client ⇄ xai-server (`wss://api.x.ai/v1/stt`). Hop 1 = typed `stt_user_*` events (audio = base64 PCM16LE in `stt_user_binary_frame`); hop 2 = raw binary. Socket field is always `ws`; provider socket is `xaiClient`.

### Server (`apps/ws-server`)
