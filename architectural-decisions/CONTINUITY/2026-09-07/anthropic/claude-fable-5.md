## 5. Hard-won wire truths (do not re-litigate)

- Files API uploads expire in **48h** — a 403 `permission_denied "access the content"` on a document URI means expired file, NOT a bad key (re-upload; probe scripts take the URI as $2).
- Lyria streams: ONE model_output step, full lyrics as one TextDelta, then AudioDelta `{mime_type:"audio/mpeg", data}` (C2PA-signed mp3); zero thought tokens. `audio/wav` unsupported despite SDK types + docs.
- Google added lyria safety filters ~Sep 2026 (output "no longer slaps as hard").
- Veo models: keep as-is, don't remove/deepen — omni (`gemini-omni-flash-preview`) is the future video lane via interactions `generation_config.video_config`.
- Interactions model ids: probe used `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (GA id) but the catalog's `-preview` id works live through the flipped lane.
- fflate: useless for audio (mp3/ID3/WAV/RIFF all uncompressed) and video metadata (mp4 boxes/EBML raw) except legacy QuickTime `cmov` atoms; earns its keep only in image (PNG zTXt/iCCP) and PDF lanes.
- max_tokens/max_output_tokens pass-through = legacy noise, backend-only, always undefined from clients; don't re-flag against the CLAUDE.md no-caps rule and don't gut unprompted.
